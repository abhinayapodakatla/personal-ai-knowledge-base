Generative AI

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content.

It can generate text, images, audio, video, and code.

Large Language Models are AI models that can understand and generate human language.

Examples of generative AI applications include chatbots, text generation, code generation, image generation, and document summarization.

Generative AI can be used in education, healthcare, software development, business, and content creation.

AI models learn patterns from large amounts of data.
